## 🎯 What I'm looking for

Technical product roles are my main focus: **technical product manager, APM and PM**. I'm especially keen on AI products and automation, and I'm happy to work on whatever problem needs a PM who can write the spec, test the thing and explain it clearly.

## 🧭 How I work as a product manager

- **Discovery and framing:** turn a vague complaint into a clear problem, a user, and a success metric.
- **Specs and prioritisation:** PRDs, trade-offs, roadmap choices and launch plans that engineering can estimate.
- **Quality as a product job:** I own release readiness. I run sanity passes, classify every issue as working, dev issue or blocker, and say plainly whether something is safe to ship.
- **Triage:** I separate bugs from configuration problems and product decisions, then route each to the right owner with evidence.
- **Data-driven:** data-readiness audits tied to the KPIs they feed, and AI evaluations that turn failing test scores into a ranked list of product problems.
- **Communication:** narrated demo videos, "how it works" explainers and handover docs that non-technical stakeholders can act on.

## 🤖 How I use AI

AI is part of my daily product workflow, not a side interest.

- **Prototyping:** I build AI agents and automations on no-code platforms to test an idea or show a demo, often within a single meeting cycle.
- **Connecting tools:** email, sheets, databases and APIs wired together with triggers, loops and approvals.
- **Testing agents like a product:** cost per reply, output format, tool behaviour and failure patterns, before a customer sees them.
- **Reusable skills:** if I do a task twice, I turn it into an AI skill my team can run without me.
